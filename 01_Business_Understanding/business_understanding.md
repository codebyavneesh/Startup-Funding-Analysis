1. Problem Statement
The Indian startup ecosystem is growing rapidly. However, for a new founder, it is difficult to know which sector, city, or stage is best to start a company.

2. Objective
The goal of this project is to analyze Indian startup funding data (2015–2017) to help an early-stage founder make data-driven decisions.

3. Key Business Questions
Which sectors have the highest number of early-stage (Seed / Angel) funding deals?

Which cities are emerging as new startup hubs (beyond Bangalore and Mumbai)?

Which investors are most active in early-stage funding?

What is the month-over-month and year-over-year funding trend?

4. Stakeholder
Primary Stakeholder: An early-stage founder who wants to start a company and raise seed/angel funding.

5. Success Criteria
The analysis will be successful if it can clearly answer:

"Where should a founder start their company (city & sector)?"

"What type of investors should they target?"
